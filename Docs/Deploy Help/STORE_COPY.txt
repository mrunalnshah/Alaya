FULL DESCRIPTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Most money apps track what you spent. Alaya knows what you have.

Your expenses, your groceries, your appliances, your recipes — one record of
your whole household, and all of it stays on your phone. No account. No cloud.
No one watching.

WHAT IT DOES

• Money — Log an expense in about eight seconds: amount, what it was for,
  done. Income, transfers, and full itemised purchases when you want them.
  Multiple accounts, multiple currencies, and Indian lakh-crore formatting
  throughout.

• Inventory — What's in the house, how much of it, and when it expires.
  Batches with separate expiry dates, so the older milk gets used first.

• Recipes that read your kitchen — Add a recipe and Alaya tells you whether
  you can make it right now from what you have. Measure in tablespoons,
  teaspoons or cups; it converts to grams using the weight you set once per
  ingredient. Cook it, and the stock comes off your shelves automatically.

• Shopping lists — Built from what's running low, and turned into a purchase
  in one step when you get home.

• Recurring bills — Rent, subscriptions, the milkman. Alaya knows what's due
  and reminds you before it is.

• Appliances and upkeep — Warranties, service dates, who to call. It tells you
  the AC is due before the summer, not after.

• Analytics — Where the money went, by month, by category, by account. Tap any
  number to see the transactions behind it.

BUILT DIFFERENTLY

No account. There is no sign-up, no email, no password to forget. Open the app
and use it.

Works with no internet, forever. Not "offline mode" — offline is the only mode.
Nothing syncs because there is nowhere to sync to.

Your data is yours. One tap exports a full backup file you can put anywhere.
Nothing is uploaded, nothing is analysed, nothing is sold.

It admits what it doesn't know. When Alaya can't work something out — a
conversion it has no factor for — it says so instead of guessing. A wrong
number you trust is worse than no number.

Locks with a PIN or your fingerprint. Readable at double text size on a small
screen. Full screen-reader support.

FREE, AND HONEST ABOUT IT

Every feature above is free and always will be. There is an optional Support Us
screen with a small ad and a tip jar for anybody who wants to chip in. Nothing
is gated, nothing expires, and there is no subscription.

Alaya — आलय — is Sanskrit for the place where things are kept. It is the second
half of Himalaya: hima, snow, and alaya, abode. Your things stay in the abode.

Made by WildeWulf.
