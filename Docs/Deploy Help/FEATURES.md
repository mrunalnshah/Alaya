# What Alaya does

A record of a household — its money, its things, its food and its upkeep. Everything on this page works
with no internet connection, no account and no data leaving your phone.

---

## Money

**Log an expense in about eight seconds.** Amount, what it was for, save. That is the whole required
path — one field is mandatory and everything else has a sensible default.

Tap **Save and add details** instead and the same entry opens in the full editor, where you can set the
account, the payment method, tags, individual line items, or a payee.

- **Expenses, income and transfers** between your own accounts
- **Itemised purchases** — a grocery bill broken into the things in the bag, each one able to update your
  inventory
- **Multiple accounts** — cash, bank, wallet, card
- **Multiple currencies**, with the exchange rate *frozen at the moment of the transaction* so history
  never rewrites itself when rates move
- **Indian number formatting** — ₹1,00,000 rather than ₹100,000, throughout
- **Trash** — a deleted transaction is recoverable, not gone

Anything saved through the fast path is flagged **needs review**, so a shortcut never quietly becomes
bad data.

## Inventory

What is in the house, how much of it, and when it goes off.

- **Batches with their own expiry dates.** Two litres of milk bought a week apart are two batches, and
  the older one is used first
- **Consume, waste or adjust** — three different things, recorded differently, so "we ate it" and "it went
  off" don't look the same later
- **Low-stock thresholds** per item, feeding the shopping list
- **Weight, volume or count** — each item measured the way it actually comes
- **Full movement history** — every change to a stock level, with a reason

## Recipes that read your kitchen

The part no other app does.

**Add a recipe and Alaya tells you whether you can make it right now.** Each ingredient shows its own
verdict against your actual stock — enough, short, none left, or *can't tell*.

**Measure in the units you cook in.** Teaspoons, tablespoons and cups, with the fractions a measuring
drawer actually has — ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾ — as one tap each.

**Spoons convert to grams.** Set once per ingredient how much one tablespoon of it weighs — flour about
8 g, sugar 12 g, oil 14 g — and Alaya works out teaspoons, cups and millilitres from that single number.
The row shows you the result as you type: *3 ½ tablespoon = 28 g*.

**Cook it, and your shelves update.** The ingredients come out of stock, oldest batch first, in one
transaction. If you are short of something it refuses and tells you which — a half-applied deduction is
worse than none.

**Scale servings** and every amount rescales, rounding up so it never claims you have enough when you
don't.

**It says when it can't tell.** An ingredient you don't track — salt, water — or one measured in a way
that can't be converted reads as *can't tell*, not *can't cook*. A confident wrong answer is worse than
an honest gap.

## Shopping

- Lists built from **what is actually running low**
- **Turn a finished list into a purchase** in one step, which creates the transaction and updates stock
  together
- Free-text items alongside catalogued ones, because not everything needs to be in a database

---

## Recurring

Rent, subscriptions, the milkman, the gas cylinder.

- Any interval — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N of those
- **Knows what is due**, and reminds you before it is
- Confirm an occurrence and it becomes a real transaction; skip one and it stays skipped
- Pause a template without deleting its history

---

## Appliances and upkeep

- **Warranties**, with the end date and who provided it
- **Service intervals** — it tells you the AC is due *before* the summer
- **Service records** with what was done and what it cost
- **Who to call**, kept with the thing rather than in your contacts
- Service providers can be recorded as assets too — the plumber is a thing you maintain a relationship
  with

---

## Analytics

- Where the money went, by month, by category, by account
- **Tap any number to see the transactions behind it.** No figure in the app is a dead end
- Trends over time, and the same numbers in your home currency when several are in play
- Approximate figures are **marked as approximate**, never presented as exact

---

## Calendar

Every dated thing in one month view — transactions, bills due, service dates, expiries. Tap a day for
what happened or what is coming.

---

## Reminders

- Daily digest at a time you choose
- Six kinds you can switch on separately: bills due, low stock, expiring food, warranty ending, service
  due, recurring items
- **All off by default.** Nothing notifies you until you ask it to
- No exact alarms, so the app never fights your battery settings

---

## Your data

**Backup** — one tap writes a complete file wherever you choose: your Downloads, Drive, a USB stick, a
message to yourself. It is a real file you own.

**Restore** — from any backup, with a clear warning at every stage about what will be replaced.

**Nothing is uploaded.** There is no server. The backup goes where you send it and nowhere else.

Alaya cannot be included in Android's automatic cloud backup — deliberately disabled — so your financial
records never end up in a Google Drive backup you forgot was running.

---

## Privacy and security

- **No account.** No email, no password, no sign-up screen
- **No internet needed, ever.** The only network use is an optional exchange-rate lookup and the Support
  Us screen
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data sharing**
- **PIN or fingerprint lock**, with an auto-lock timeout you set
- The lock is **not** restored from a backup, so a backup file can't carry someone else's PIN onto your
  phone

---

## Accessibility and comfort

- Readable at **double the system text size** on a 320dp screen — the whole app, not just the main screens
- **Full screen-reader support**, with rows read as one thing rather than five fragments
- **Colour is never the only signal** — anything shown in colour is also stated in words
- Five colour palettes, light and dark
- Honours your system reduced-motion setting by removing animation, not shortening it

## Free

Every feature on this page is free and will stay free. There is an optional **Support Us** screen with one
ad and a tip jar for anyone who wants to contribute. Nothing is gated, nothing expires, there is no
subscription and no upgrade prompt anywhere else in the app.
