# Privacy Policy — Alaya

**Last updated: 10 August 2026**

---

## The short version

Alaya keeps everything on your device. There is no account, no server, and no way for us to see your data
— not because we promise not to look, but because it never reaches us.

We collect nothing. Google, through the advertising in the optional Support Us screen, does collect
something, and this policy tells you exactly what and how to stop it.

## What Alaya stores, and where

Everything you enter — transactions, accounts, inventory, recipes, assets, notes, settings — is stored in a
database file **inside Alaya's private storage on your device**. Other apps cannot read it. We cannot read
it. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Alaya is **excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup**, deliberately. Your financial records will not
appear in a Google Drive backup you had forgotten was running.

**Backups you create are yours.** When you tap Backup, Alaya writes a file to the location *you* pick. Where
it goes after that is entirely your choice — a folder on the phone, a cloud drive, a message to yourself.
Nothing is sent automatically.

**The backup file is not encrypted.** The app tells you this every time you export one. Treat it like a
document containing your financial records, because that is what it is.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

No account, no email address, no name, no phone number. No usage analytics, no crash reporting, no
telemetry. We have no server, so there is nowhere for your data to arrive.

We cannot tell you how many people use Alaya beyond what Google Play reports about installs, and we cannot
tell you what any of them did in it.

## When Alaya uses the internet

Two things, both optional, and neither carries your data:

**Exchange rates.** If you use more than one currency, Alaya can fetch published rates. The request asks for
rates and sends nothing about you or your transactions. Turn it off in Settings and the app works entirely
offline.

**The Support Us screen.** If you open it, Google's Mobile Ads SDK loads an advert. This is the only place in
Alaya where a third party is involved, and you have to navigate to it deliberately.

**Everywhere else in the app works with no connection at all.** Aeroplane mode changes nothing except those
two things.

## Advertising, and how to limit it

The Support Us screen shows adverts through **Google AdMob**. When you open it, Google may access:

- Your device's **advertising ID**
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Device and app information

**Google is the data controller for this**, not us. We receive no personal data from it — only an aggregate
count of impressions and earnings.

**In the EEA, UK and Switzerland** you are shown a consent form before any advert loads. If you decline,
Alaya requests no advert at all. Declining costs you nothing; the app is identical.

**Anywhere in the world** you can limit this outside Alaya:
**Android Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID.**

Google's own policy: **https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads**

If you never open the Support Us screen, **no advert is ever requested and no advertising ID is ever
read.**

## Payments

The Support Us screen offers an optional one-time tip through Google Play Billing. **Google processes the
payment.** We never see your card details, and we receive only the confirmation that a purchase happened.

Nothing in Alaya is gated behind it. Tipping unlocks nothing, because nothing is locked.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | Can you refuse? |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Reminders you have switched on | Yes. Alaya asks only when you enable your first reminder, never at launch. Refuse and everything else works |
| **Biometric** | Optional fingerprint unlock | Yes. Use a PIN, or no lock at all |
| **Advertising ID** | Required by the ads SDK, used only in Support Us | Delete it in Android settings, or never open that screen |
| **Internet** | Exchange rates and adverts only | Both optional |

Alaya asks for **no storage permission**. Backups use Android's document picker, which grants access to the
one file you chose and nothing else.

Alaya asks for **no location, camera, contacts, microphone or SMS permission.** It does not have them and
cannot request them.

## Children

Alaya is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. It contains no social features,
no user-generated content visible to others, and no messaging.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, you already have complete control:

- **Access it** — it is all on your screen
- **Export it** — Settings › Data › Backup produces a full copy
- **Delete it** — uninstalling Alaya removes the database entirely. There is no copy anywhere else, and
  nothing for you to request from us

There is no data-deletion request to file, because there is no data of yours in our possession.
