# Alaya · ARCH 4 — Decisions & Anomalies

Why the architecture looks the way it does. Reference document — rarely attached to a code
prompt, but read it before overruling anything.

---

## 1. What changed from your description, and why

Your description was unusually complete. Most of what follows is not "features I removed" —
it is **category errors fixed**. A category error in a finance schema does not surface as a
bug; it surfaces six months in as *"why don't my totals add up"*, and by then it is a
migration, not a fix.

Six changes carry almost all the risk reduction:

| # | Change | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Split "payment option" into **Account** (where money sits) + **PaymentMethod** (how it moved) + **Payee** (who) | Your deposit list mixed all three: `Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Salary`. Cash is a container, UPI is a rail, Salary is a source. Without the split, "Total Available Funds" is unanswerable. |
| **C2** | `transfer` is its own transaction kind, **not** a withdrawal | Moving ₹5,000 Cash → Bank must not reduce net worth. As specced, it would. |
| **C3** | Money as **integer minor units**, never `double` | `0.1 + 0.2 != 0.3`. Conversion across 10k transactions gives you drifting totals you can never explain to a user. |
| **C4** | Quantity as **integer milli-base-units** | Same reason, plus it lets you record `0.5 pc` and `0.25 g` without floats. |
| **C5** | **Assets** (Service Manager) are a separate entity from **Items** (Inventory) | A TV is not consumable stock. As specced, electronics went to both and neither owned the truth. |
| **C6** | Inventory stock is an **append-only movement ledger** with a cached balance | This is the rule you already stated for money — "calculate from transactions". It has to apply to stock too, or "deduct 100 g" becomes unauditable and un-undoable. |

### 1.1 Version 2.0 — encryption removed

Removing encryption did more than delete one section. It **simplified three subsystems and
dissolved one contradiction**:

| Area | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| DB open | DEK/KEK envelope, `PRAGMA key`, `PRAGMA cipher` assert, `sqlite3mc` build hook, key vault + sidecar JSON | Plain `driftDatabase()`. One file, no crypto. |
| PIN | Derived the encryption key; changing it meant re-wrapping keys; a lost PIN + phrase meant **permanent data loss** | A UI gate with a hashed credential. Lost PIN is recoverable, and worst case is recoverable from a backup. |
| Recovery | 12-word BIP39 mnemonic, forced re-entry of 3 words, legal-grade warning copy | A 10-character recovery code, one checkbox. `bip39` dependency dropped. |
| Backup | NDJSON bundle + manifest + checksum + bundle codec, because `VACUUM INTO` from an encrypted connection had unverified behaviour | `VACUUM INTO` — one SQL statement. Restore is `ATTACH` + upsert. **This is literally what you originally asked for** ("SQL file directly") and it only became easy once encryption was gone. |
| Threat model | Encrypted at rest but plaintext shareable backups — opposite models in one app | Consistent: plaintext everywhere, stated plainly. |

**But it created one new Severity 1 anomaly** — see A42. Android's auto-backup is on by
default and would quietly upload the plaintext financial database to Google Drive.
`android:allowBackup="false"` is now the single most important line in the manifest.

### 1.2 Where your spec contradicts itself

| Where | Contradiction | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Lines 38 vs 43/106 | Show `2 kg` and `250 g` separately, **or** show the summed `2 kg 450 g`? | Both are satisfiable: Item sums, Batch itemises. ARCH_1 §5.4. Also your worked example has an arithmetic slip — `250+2000+1500+700 = 4450 g` → **4 kg 450 g**, not 2 kg 450 g. Now a required test case. |
| Line 110 vs 144–145 | "Internet only for currency" vs rewarded ads + Play Billing | Explicit three-entry network allowlist. ARCH_3 §1.4. |
| Lines 148–149 vs 152–153 | Encrypted DB (PIN derives key) vs plaintext backups | **Dissolved by your v2 decision.** Now consistent. |

### 1.3 A correction to your tech research

> `sqlcipher_flutter_libs` version 0.7.0 is a deliberate no-op — the package is obsolete after
> upgrading to `sqlite3` 3.x. `sqlite3_flutter_libs` must also not be added. This no longer
> affects us (no encryption), but the same trap applies to the plain build: with `sqlite3` 3.x
> the SQLite binary arrives via build hooks automatically, and adding either libs package
> breaks the build. Nearly every tutorial online is still wrong about this.

## 2. Anomaly register

41 original findings + 4 introduced by the v2 encryption change. Sorted by blast radius.

### 2.1 Severity 1 — would corrupt data or produce wrong numbers

| ID | Feature | Anomaly | Resolution | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Deposit | `Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Salary` are three concepts in one list | `accounts` + `payment_methods` + `payees`. Salary becomes a seeded deposit tag + a payee. UI still shows one flat chip row. | ARCH_2 §4 |
| A02 | Withdrawal | "Transfer money" as a withdrawal destroys net worth on self-transfers | `kind = transfer` with both accounts. Excluded from deposit/withdrawal totals. UI asks "my own account" vs "someone else"; the latter is a real withdrawal with a payee. | ARCH_2 §12.1 |
| A03 | Global | No opening balance. A new user's real ₹40,000 in cash is invisible forever | `accounts.openingBalanceMinor` + date, captured in onboarding. Plus `adjustmentIncrease/Decrease` kinds for reconciliation. | ARCH_2 §4 |
| A04 | Global | Money as `double` | `Money(int minor, code)`; `decimalDigits` per currency; one rounding site | L1 |
| A05 | Inventory | Quantity as `double`; can't express `0.5 pc` | `Qty(int milliBase, category)` | L2 |
| A06 | Inventory | "merge if units match" — item identity undefined | Identity `(normalizedName, unitCategory)` via partial unique index | ARCH_2 §11 |
| A07 | Inventory | Silent fuzzy merging corrupts data (`tomatoes`/`tomato`/`टमाटर`) | Deterministic normalizer auto-merges **exact** matches only. Near-matches surface a suggestion chip, never auto-merge. | `normalizer.dart` |
| A08 | Inventory | "deduct 100 g from 1 kg" — which batch? | **FEFO**, falling back to FIFO for no-expiry batches. User override. Multi-batch splits into multiple movements. | Phase 4B |
| A09 | Inventory | Mutable stock number = unauditable, no undo, no waste data | Append-only `stock_movements`; cached remaining; `v_batch_stock_check` reconciler | L3 |
| A10 | Expense ↔ Inv | Deleting a grocery receipt would delete food already eaten | Never cascades. Detaches (`origin = detached`). Offers removal only if the batch is provably untouched. | ARCH_3 §4.1 |
| A11 | Expense | ₹480 of lines on a ₹500 transaction | Transaction amount is truth; lines optional; `unallocatedMinor` chip; never auto-balanced | `v_transaction_allocation` |
| A12 | Electronics | A TV pushed to both Inventory and Service; neither owns it | `transaction_lines.destination ∈ {none,inventory,asset,recurring}`; one line → at most one artefact, recorded | ARCH_2 §4.2 |
| A13 | Recurring | Jan 31 monthly → Feb 28 → Mar 28, permanently wrong | Store `anchorDayOfMonth`, **clamp at render**, never mutate | Phase 4B |
| A14 | Recurring | App unopened 3 months — auto-create 3 payments? | Never. Occurrences materialise lazily as `due`; overdue is derived. Money only from an explicit tap. | Phase 4B |
| A15 | Currency | "closest rate to date" undefined; Frankfurter has no weekend rows | **Greatest `rateDateKey <= D`**; else earliest + `approximate`. Never interpolate. | ARCH_3 §1.3 |
| A16 | Currency | Writing the converted amount back destroys the original | `original*` immutable; totals computed at read time; freeze writes separate columns | L9 |
| A19 | Soft delete | Unique index + soft delete: delete "Tomato", re-add → constraint violation | All uniqueness **partial**: `WHERE deleted_at IS NULL`, in raw SQL | ARCH_2 §11 |
| A20 | Soft delete | One forgotten `deletedAt IS NULL` = silently wrong money | Repositories may only read views; base-table reads are a review reject | L7 |
| **A42** | **v2** | **`android:allowBackup` defaults to true — Android would upload the plaintext financial DB to Google Drive** | **`allowBackup="false"` + `dataExtractionRules` excluding everything. Also blocks `adb backup`. Confirmed missing from the real project's manifest on 2026-07-28; moved from Phase 8B to Phase 1A because the DB lands in 1B.** | **ARCH_3 §2.4, Phase 1A** |

**Withdrawn in v2:** A17 (PIN-derived key forces full-DB rekey) and A18 (key material stored in
the database it unlocks). Both existed only because of encryption.

### 2.3 Deliberate non-goals

Recorded so they don't get re-litigated: no cloud sync, no bank/SMS auto-import, no multi-user
or shared household accounts, no budgets or envelopes, no OCR receipt scanning, no
lending/investment tracking (which also keeps you outside Play's Financial Services policy),
no web or iOS. Attachments get a table in Phase 1B so there is no later migration, but no UI
until Phase 8B.

than tool-generation, every time — `flutter pub add` cannot produce a stale floor, and a human
typing `^0.19.0` for a `0.x` package can.

## 6. Phase 7A decisions and closures

### 6.1 Closed

**§5.1 item 15 — `CalendarRepository` has no implementation.** Closed. `calendarDaoProvider`,
`calendarRepositoryProvider` and `calendarAggregatorProvider` exist, and the aggregator now serves both the
calendar screen and the dashboard.

**The dashboard's hand-rolled upcoming shortlist.** Retired. `upcomingProvider` delegates to
`calendarAggregatorProvider`, which fixed two live defects it had been hiding: service dues recorded
against a **service record** never appeared, and expiring batches were titled `batch.id` — a UUID where the
calendar's view `COALESCE`s the item name. Severity now means the same thing on both screens.

### 6.2 Decisions taken

**Recurring horizon: two years, flat.** `materialiseUpTo` was only ever called with `clock.today()`, so
**no future occurrence row existed at all** and the calendar's `recurringDue` arm could show nothing beyond
today. `recurringHorizonProvider` now materialises two years once per session, awaited by both the month
feed and the dashboard card. Given `maxOccurrencesPerPass = 240`, monthly and weekly templates reach the
full two years in one pass; a daily template reaches about eight months, which is the cap doing its job. A
data-driven horizon — out to the furthest warranty or service — was considered and rejected: it needs a
`max()` across four tables, and the only way to *see* a date that far out is to browse to it.

**Span totals read transactions, not the calendar feed.** `v_calendar_events` selects
`original_amount_minor` as an unsigned magnitude and no `kind`, so deposits and withdrawals are
indistinguishable there. Adding `kind` to the view would have meant a schema version bump and a migration
for numbers `TransactionRepository.watchByDateRange` already provides. **No schema change was made in 7A.**

**Transfers are excluded from span totals.** Money moving between your own accounts is neither spending nor
income; counting it as both doubles the totals of anyone who moves money in order to save it.

**Span totals cover one currency.** The first currency seen names the result and others are skipped, not
added. Summing minor units across currencies yields a number that looks right and is not; converting needs
the frozen-rate path that belongs to analytics.

**Shopping targets stay out of the dashboard's upcoming card, deliberately.** Every kind it carries is
consequential if ignored — a bill goes late, a service lapses, a warranty dies, food spoils. A shopping
target is self-imposed, and a weekly shop would land in almost every fortnight, making it the one row
always present and therefore the one carrying no signal. The calendar is where plans belong.

**The dashboard month grid is locked to its month.** `availableGestures: none` alone is insufficient:
`firstDay`/`lastDay` spanning years leave the underlying `PageView` with neighbouring pages a fling can
still reach. `lockedToMonth` narrows the bounds so the pages do not exist.

**Range selection is a long-press on a day, not a mode button.** `table_calendar`'s own
`rangeSelectionMode` was declined: it routes range cells to `rangeStartBuilder`/`withinRangeBuilder`/
`rangeEndBuilder`, bypassing the four builders the app owns and losing the severity dots on every day
inside a range. The two dates live in the screen; `onDayLongPressed` is the trigger.

### 6.3 Open

**`v_calendar_events` has no `parent_ref_id`.** Three event types cannot deep-link to their record:
`inventoryBatch` needs its item id, `serviceRecord` needs its asset id, and `recurringOccurrence` has no
route at all. All three fall back to the owning module. Closing it is a Phase 2A view change; 7B wants it
too.

**Recurring beyond the two-year horizon is invisible.** A consequence of the decision above, not a bug.

**A dashboard provider imports a calendar-feature provider.** `upcomingProvider` and `DashboardCalendar`
both read `lib/features/calendar/providers/calendar_providers.dart`. This is the honest consequence of one
engine serving two screens, but if ARCH_1 §6 forbids feature-to-feature imports then
`recurringHorizonProvider` and `currentMonthProvider` belong in `lib/app/providers/`.

**`overflow_diagnostic_test.dart` belongs to no phase document** and prints on every run.

**`seed_test.dart` constructs `AlayaDatabase` three times per run**, producing drift's
multiple-database warning.

**The home action is currently unconditional**, so it appears on the dashboard where it is redundant. It
was `if (!atDashboard)` and never rendered because of the pathless-`ShellRoute` fault in ARCH_5 U27;
restoring the condition is one line once its visibility is confirmed on a device.

**The calendar screen's own day cells are 45.7dp at 320dp** and would fail
`androidTapTargetGuideline` — its suite has never checked tap targets, so it passes on absence of coverage
rather than compliance. See ARCH_5 U30 for why no seven-column grid can comply.
